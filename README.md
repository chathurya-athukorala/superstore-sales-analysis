Superstore Sales Analysis

A data analysis project exploring sales and profitability trends using the Kaggle Superstore dataset (2014–2017), covering ~10,000 order records across four U.S. regions.

Objective:
To identify what drives sales, profit and losses across categories, regions, and discount levels, and translate the findings into clear, actionable business recommendations.

Key Insights:
Sales grew 51% and profit grew 89% from 2014 to 2017.
September–December accounts for 52% of annual sales, highlighting strong seasonality.
Furniture generates high sales but near-zero profit margin (2.5%), driven mainly by Tables and Bookcases.
Discounts above 20% resulted in a combined loss of over $135K.
The Central region has the weakest profit margin (7.9%), linked to higher average discounting.

Tools & Skills:
Excel – data cleaning and exploration
PowerPoint – data storytelling and presentation design

Outcome:
A structured, presentation-ready analysis with clear recommendations, including a discount policy review and category-level pricing adjustments.
